# The Bitter Lesson (Sutton, 2019)

One-page essay surveying 70 years of AI research (chess, Go, speech, vision) to argue that **general methods leveraging computation win over methods that bake in human domain knowledge**, because Moore's law keeps widening the compute gap. Sutton names the two primitives that scale arbitrarily with compute: **search** and **learning**. Everything else — clever encodings of how humans think the problem should be solved — eventually plateaus.
