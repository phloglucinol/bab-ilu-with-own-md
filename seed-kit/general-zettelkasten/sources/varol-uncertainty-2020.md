# Think Like a Rocket Scientist (Varol, 2020)

Popular-nonfiction treatment of space-industry engineering heuristics, reframed as a general thinking toolkit. Chapter 1 (*Dancing with Uncertainty*) is the one Bab-ilu's zettelkasten `examples.md` extracts under this lens.

Varol's Chapter 1 argument: our desire for certainty locks us into searching under the streetlight (where the light is, not where the keys are); the real epistemic blocker is *self-perceived expertise* (Boorstin) rather than ignorance; we fill the void under uncertainty with *stories* (Kahneman) that feel like truth. Breakthroughs require stepping outside the lit circle.

See [[human-centrism-as-epistemic-trap]] for the cross-source synthesis with Sutton's bitter-lesson — the two books attack the same trap at different layers (Varol = cognitive, Sutton = methodological).
