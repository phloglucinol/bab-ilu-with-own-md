# Drunkard Under Streetlight

The heuristic story Varol opens Chapter 1 with: a man searches for his lost keys under a streetlight not because he dropped them there, but because that's where the light is. The story diagnoses a pattern — we search in the region of certainty (where we already have tools / concepts / data) instead of the region where the answer actually lives (usually off the lit circle).

Not a Varol original — Feynman told a version, and it's attributed earlier still — but Varol's move is to embed it in a framework: the streetlight is *certainty*, and the reason we refuse to leave it is [[illusion-of-knowledge]] combined with the cognitive cost of admitting we don't yet know where to look.
