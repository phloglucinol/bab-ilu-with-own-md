# Epistemic Humility Under Uncertainty

A cluster of concepts that together describe *why self-certainty is the primary epistemic blocker, not ignorance*. Emerges from pairing Varol's ch.1 framework with Sutton's bitter-lesson when both are read under this lens.

The cluster spans ≥2 Bloom levels (per this lens's cluster-validity rule):
- **Understand** — [[drunkard-under-streetlight]], [[illusion-of-knowledge]] (mechanism identification)
- **Evaluate** — [[narrative-filling]], [[human-centrism-as-epistemic-trap]] (judging where these mechanisms are actively blocking)
- **Apply** (latent) — [[uncertainty-literacy]] (the practical posture that follows)

## What binds the members

Each member names a specific *move* that substitutes confidence for evidence:
- `drunkard-under-streetlight` — searching where you already are instead of where the answer is
- `illusion-of-knowledge` — mistaking familiarity for understanding (Boorstin's phrase, verbatim)
- `narrative-filling` — manufacturing a story to close an epistemic void (Kahneman)
- `human-centrism-as-epistemic-trap` — the methodological generalization across Sutton + Varol
- `uncertainty-literacy` — the counter-posture: treating ambiguity as signal, not noise
