# 笔记反模式 — Progressive Summary Template

## Source
- Book / paper / conversation being summarized: *How to Take Smart Notes* — Sönke Ahrens (2017), Chapter 8: "Give Each Idea an Address"
- Vault anchor: [[wikilink-is-not-hyperlink]]

## Layer 1 — Bold key sentences (raw highlighting)
When reading the source, bold the sentences that answer what [[literature-note]] is asking: at what point does note-taking stop being knowledge-building and become archive-filling?

Not "cautionary" sentences — diagnostic ones. The test: does this sentence name a failure mode precisely enough that you could recognize it in your own vault tomorrow?

Example pull-quote from Ahrens:
> "**Most students collect information. The Zettelkasten method asks you to collect connections — and a connection without both endpoints understood is not a connection, it is a placeholder.**"

A second candidate:
> "**A literature note that paraphrases is worse than a quotation: it gives you the author's thought in your handwriting, making it easy to mistake their understanding for yours.**"

## Layer 2 — Bold fragments inside Layer 1
From the bolded sentences above, pick 3–5 fragments that carry the conceptual weight. Drop connective tissue.

- **collect connections, not information**
- **a connection without both endpoints understood**
- **placeholder**
- **the author's thought in your handwriting**
- **mistake their understanding for yours**

## Layer 3 — One-sentence thesis
Anti-patterns in Zettelkasten practice share a common structure: they produce the *appearance* of knowledge work — filled vaults, long [[literature-note]] files, dense link graphs — while bypassing the moment of genuine understanding that [[link-as-knowledge-unit]] requires.

## Traps
- Layer 3 that lists anti-patterns instead of diagnosing their shared root cause
- Using this template to audit someone else's vault — it is a self-diagnostic, not a judgment tool
- Stopping at identification: naming an anti-pattern without specifying the corrective action leaves the note at Bloom's "remember" level instead of "evaluate"
