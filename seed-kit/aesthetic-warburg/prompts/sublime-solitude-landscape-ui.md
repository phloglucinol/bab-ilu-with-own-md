# Sublime Solitude Landscape — UI surface (崇高孤独作为界面尺度)

## Subject
A landing page or onboarding screen whose sole interactive element is placed small and low, against a vast, largely-empty upper field.
The user's first impression is scale — the product's claim lives in the negative space above, not in the button.
One headline, one sub-line, one call-to-action.
No feature grid below the fold on first paint.

## Style
Friedrich 1808 (Monk by the Sea, Alte Nationalgalerie A I 486) horizontal-band discipline / Rothko's color-field inheritance of Friedrich's emptied horizon / Kubrick 1968 (2001: A Space Odyssey) Star Gate and monolith shot as 20th-century Rückenfigur-against-sublime.
The lineage test: the user is the small standing figure; the product is the sea-and-sky.

## Composition
Three stacked horizontal bands: top 60% atmospheric field (gradient, photograph, or tone), middle 10% horizon line (literal or typographic baseline), bottom 30% breathing ground containing the small interactive element.
The CTA sits at roughly 1/4 width, left-of-center, vertically placed so it echoes Friedrich's off-center monk.
Color key: deep oklch(32% 0.04 240) upper band, oklch(88% 0.01 240) ground, single oklch(64% 0.14 45) warm accent reserved for the CTA only.

## Motion
Ambient motion allowed only in the upper atmospheric band — a slow gradient drift of 20–40 second cycle, imperceptible unless stared at. The CTA and text stay still.
Reduced-motion disables the drift entirely; the composition remains legible.

## Notes
Do NOT generate: centered hero with radial gradient blob behind the headline, bento grids of feature cards, testimonial carousels, animated scroll prompts, parallax layers, emoji in the CTA, multi-column above the fold. This Pathosformel is about scale contrast — if the button is the hero, the form is broken.
