# Quiet Interior Light — still image (静室之光作为静帧)

## Subject
A single unadorned room: plaster wall, wooden floor, one window on the left wall admitting light from outside the frame.
One figure — or no figure — caught inside a suspended everyday action (pouring, reading a letter, standing still, or absent entirely with only a half-open door).
Furniture count minimal: a table, a chair, one ceramic object. Surfaces are matte, not polished.
The window is translucent, not transparent — the outside is not readable.

## Style
Vermeer 1657 (Milkmaid, Rijksmuseum SK-A-2344) pigment restraint and granular tactile realism / Hammershøi c.1900 (Interior, Tate N05032) depopulated chamber and grey-bone palette / Deakins 2019 (1917 farmhouse dawn) cold-blue dawn temperature and lens-weighted depth.
The continuity is four centuries of northern-European window discipline; each node removed something — Vermeer the drama, Hammershøi the figure, Deakins the safety.

## Composition
Window at left, 20–30% of frame width. Figure (if present) at right-center, three-quarter turned toward the light source, face only half-lit.
Horizon inside the frame: the table edge or the baseboard line, held horizontal.
Negative space upper-right: 30% of the canvas is simply wall and air. Do not fill it.
Color key: bone white, flax linen, cold milk-blue from the window, a single warm ochre from the figure's garment or a wooden surface. Nothing saturated.

## Motion
Not applicable for still image — but the composition must imply that motion has just stopped or is about to begin. No frozen action; held action.

## Notes
Do NOT generate: romantic candlelit interiors, cozy golden hour, fireplace scenes, Pinterest-farmhouse decor, cluttered still-lifes, HDR contrast, lens flare, wide-angle distortion, multiple light sources. This Pathosformel is austere and monocular — warmth is refused.
