# Liminal Threshold Zone — still image (阈限地带作为静帧)

## Subject
A physical boundary caught mid-crossing: a bent chain-link fence with its gap facing the viewer; an overgrown doorframe with no door; a corridor whose far end disappears into soft refraction; a rail platform whose far track fades into non-standard vegetation.
No figure, or one figure seen from behind in the act of passing through — never facing camera, never stopped.
Small evidence of the world's rules being locally wrong: pooled water where there should be none; plants of a color slightly off; an object suspended without clear cause.
The image is documentary-plain, not fantastical — the strangeness is structural, not decorative.

## Style
Tarkovsky 1979 (Stalker Zone, esp. the fence-crossing and the overgrown rail scene) as origin / Strugatsky brothers' Roadside Picnic (1972) literary source discipline — the ordinary-wrong, not the spectacular-wrong / Lynch 1977 (Eraserhead industrial threshold) / Garland 2018 (Annihilation Shimmer boundary shots) as contemporary node.
Anti-citation: this is not Bosch, not Giger, not Beksinski. No body horror, no surrealist creatures. The uncanny is in the physics, not the inventory.

## Composition
Centered or near-centered boundary: fence, doorway, corridor mouth — the threshold is the subject, placed on the vertical axis.
Depth: the far side of the threshold is softer, slightly less saturated, atmospheric — but still photographic, not painted.
Negative space: above and around the threshold is empty terrain — do not fill it.
Color key: desaturated green-grey, cold-wet concrete, one anomalous hue (a pale lilac, a wrong yellow) localized to the far side. Palette count ≤ 4.

## Motion
Not applicable for still image — but the image must read as mid-transit: the viewer's eye is pulled through the threshold, not held at the near side.

## Notes
Do NOT generate: glowing portals, magical runes, monsters, ruins aesthetic, post-apocalyptic Fallout look, Silent Hill fog filters, red-and-black horror palettes, dramatic lighting through mist, VHS-horror filters, lens flares. This Pathosformel is the quiet wrong — if the image reads "horror" or "fantasy," the form is broken.
