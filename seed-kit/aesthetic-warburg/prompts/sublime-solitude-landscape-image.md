# Sublime Solitude Landscape — still image (崇高孤独作为静帧)

## Subject
A single human figure, back turned to the viewer (Rückenfigur), standing at the lower edge of a vast landscape — shoreline, ridge, salt flat, or foreign-planet horizon.
The figure occupies no more than 3–5% of the frame.
The landscape is divided by one dominant horizontal horizon; sky or atmosphere takes 60%+ of the canvas.
The figure does not gesture, does not pose — they simply face outward.

## Style
Friedrich 1808 (Monk by the Sea) as the origin — horizontal-band division and scale-violent Rückenfigur / Hudson River School 19th-century American inheritance of European sublime / Kubrick 1968 (2001: A Space Odyssey) monolith-confrontation frame and Villeneuve 2049 (Blade Runner 2049) Las Vegas-orange tableau as its 20th/21st century extension.
Citation discipline: the sublime is not a filter, it is a scale operation. The figure must be small enough that the viewer's eye reads landscape first.

## Composition
Three horizontal bands. Lowest band contains the figure offset slightly left-of-center. Middle band is a thin horizon. Upper band is atmosphere — sea, sky, nebula, mist.
Depth cue: atmospheric perspective only. No linear perspective funneling.
Color key: one dominant cool field (slate, ocean, cold grey-blue) + one atmospheric warm pulled from sun, dust, or distant fire. Palette count ≤ 3.
Frame aspect: wide — 2.39:1 or 16:9 minimum. Vertical framing breaks the form.

## Motion
Not applicable for still image — but the figure must read as having been standing for a long time before the image was captured, and as continuing to stand after. Stillness with implied duration.

## Notes
Do NOT generate: epic-fantasy matte-painting skies, rim-lit hero shots, dragon-on-mountain compositions, Instagram-desaturated moody filters, lens flares, god-rays, the figure looking over their shoulder, two or more figures, any gesture that turns this into a narrative moment. This Pathosformel is about scale, not heroism — if the figure commands the frame, the form is broken.
