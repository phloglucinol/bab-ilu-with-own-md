# Sublime Solitude Landscape — moving image (崇高孤独作为长镜头)

## Subject
A single figure at the lower edge of a vast landscape, back to camera.
The figure enters frame, walks a short distance toward the horizon, and stops. The camera does not follow past the stopping point.
Duration: 60–120 seconds. The landscape continues to exist — cloud drift, tide, blown dust — after the figure has become still.

## Style
Friedrich 1808 (Monk by the Sea) compositional origin / Kubrick 1968 (2001: A Space Odyssey) Dawn-of-Man and Star Gate durational landscape takes / Tarkovsky 1979 (Stalker Zone meadow shots) grass-wind duration / Villeneuve 2017 (Blade Runner 2049) Las Vegas orange-fog Rückenfigur / Malick 1998 (The Thin Red Line) figure-against-grass-and-sky rhythm.
The lineage refuses the sweeping drone reveal — this is the held frame, not the traversed terrain.

## Composition
Locked-off wide shot, tripod-stable. Horizon held exactly horizontal. Figure enters from screen-left or bottom, exits only by exiting the shot or by becoming still.
Frame aspect: 2.39:1 or 16:9 minimum.
Color key: one dominant atmospheric field + one distant accent (setting sun, distant fire, signal light). Graded to match the natural key; no LUT bravado.

## Motion
Static camera. The only motion belongs to the world — wind through grass, wave roll, cloud drift, blowing dust — and briefly to the figure's walk, which resolves into stillness before the shot ends.
Do not drone-orbit, do not dolly-back-and-tilt-up for a "reveal," do not speed-ramp.
Ambient environmental motion (1–3 Hz wind, 0.1 Hz cloud) is the breath of the shot.

## Notes
Do NOT generate: orchestral score swells, narration, epic drone flyovers, aerial reveals, lens flares, god-rays, the figure turning to camera, zoom-ins on the figure's face, second-figure entries, cutaways. This Pathosformel requires the audience to share the landscape's duration with the figure — if the edit imposes rhythm from outside, the form is broken.
