# Liminal Threshold Zone — moving image (阈限地带作为长镜头)

## Subject
A figure approaches a boundary (a bent fence, a doorless frame, a tunnel mouth), pauses, and passes through. The camera follows in a single unbroken take, crossing the threshold with them.
After the crossing, the camera holds on the far-side landscape for at least 15 seconds while the figure walks away and shrinks.
The shot rule: the crossing itself must not be cut. Cutting on the threshold breaks the form.

## Style
Tarkovsky 1979 (Stalker, the Zone-entry long take — the fence crossing, the handcar ride into the Zone) as the defining example / Lynch 2017 (Twin Peaks: The Return, episode 8 long-take convenience-store interior) as the most rigorous contemporary descendant / Garland 2018 (Annihilation, Shimmer approach shots) as its recent digital-age node / Bela Tarr 1994 (Sátántangó) as the formal durational ancestor.
Citation discipline: the long take is doing ontological work, not showing off. The duration is the meaning.

## Composition
Single handheld or steadicam shot. Camera at shoulder height, slightly behind and to the side of the figure, so the figure is in-frame throughout.
Threshold object centered in frame at the moment of crossing.
After the crossing, the camera slows, the figure recedes, the horizon re-establishes.
Color key: the near side is flat-documentary (grey-green, wet-concrete, muted); the far side shifts one or two degrees of chroma — imperceptibly wrong, not loud.

## Motion
Long take — 90–240 seconds minimum, no cuts.
Camera motion is breath-paced: walking speed, slight sway, no mechanical dolly smoothness.
At the exact moment of threshold-crossing, the ambient sound briefly thins out — room tone drops 3–6 dB, wind drops one frequency band. No musical cue, no swell.
After crossing: duration takes over. The audience must feel that time on the far side runs differently, without the film saying so.

## Notes
Do NOT generate: musical stingers at the moment of crossing, whispered voiceover, slow-motion on the threshold step, VFX-heavy portal effects, lens-distortion shader transitions, rapid-cut teasing of what's on the other side, jump scares, bass-drop sound design, horror-film sound design, time-lapse compression of the crossing. This Pathosformel is the held crossing — if the edit or the score dramatizes the threshold, the form is broken.
